## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Modern high-energy developer launch film
- Angle: Developers want to share what they build, but generic AI hallucinates tech stack details or leaks private keys. Build2Buzz runs locally, binds to 127.0.0.1, anchors every draft in repo facts, and enforces human-in-the-loop approval.
- Hook: "Turn GitHub Repos Into High-Impact LinkedIn Posts — Without AI hallucinations or leaked secrets."
- Outro: "Build. Showcase. Share. Your code deserves an audience that believes the facts."
